## Priority #55 evidence and validation

Both attached public log files were downloaded and read for relevant memory/exit events. The logs show a foreground
`com.aure.totk` LMKD reclaim, hundreds of other process reclaims and exhausted watermarks, with no evidence in the
report of an app SIGSEGV or Vulkan device loss. The kill-line RSS is not a complete memory census. A flat system
DMA-BUF figure does not prove a game-specific leak or prove that lowering one fixed pool will suffice.

No memory-profile fix is accepted until its production path is exercised by a regression or allocation-accounting
fixture and its actual device memory/graphics/game behavior is checked. Host-only evidence will be labeled as such.
